Main product line

Solutions for PCB micro-drill recovery

Specialized technology for different micro-drill recovery, cleaning and identification operations.

  1. Drill bit in use

    Drills the holes in the boards on the production line.

  2. Worn drill bit

    Loses its edge and collects residue with use.

  3. Recovered drill bit

    Goes back into production instead of being discarded.

  • 01

    Sharpens the worn tip MAX-500 · Regrinding

    Regrinds the tip automatically and checks the result with a camera (AOI inspection).

    MAX-500

  • 02

    Cleans the flutes A01 · Cleaning

    Removes chips, metal wire and particles left on the bit after drilling.

    A01

  • 03

    Sets the ring MAX600 · Ring setting

    The ring holds the bit at the right height in the drilling machine. The machine sets, removes and presses the ring.

    MAX600

  • 04

    Updates the control color A02 / A03 · Re-marking

    The color on top of the shank shows how many times the bit has been reground. The machine removes the old color and applies a new one.

    A02A03

A Brazilian company backed by a Hong Kong group

IVAN INTERNATIONAL TRADER GROUP LTDA is a company incorporated in Brazil and an investee company of IVAN International Trade Group Limited, headquartered in Hong Kong.

Supported by China's supply chain and the technical service resources of IVAN Hong Kong, both companies jointly develop the Brazilian and South American markets, supplying machines, equipment and materials for printed circuit board fabrication and electronics assembly.

Our service model combines international supply, technical expertise and local support, identifying each customer's production needs and offering solutions that fit their reality.
