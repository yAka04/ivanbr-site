Equipment for the PCB and PCBA industry, with local technical support in Brazil.

We are the Brazilian operation of the IVAN group, headquartered in Hong Kong. We supply machines, equipment and materials for printed circuit board fabrication and electronics assembly, connecting China's supply chain to the Brazilian and South American markets.

Two industrial segments

We serve printed circuit board fabricators and electronics assemblers separately.

PCB

Printed circuit board fabrication (PCB)

Equipment and solutions for different stages of the fabrication process, plus production materials.

  • Dry and wet process equipment
  • Automatic drill bit regrinding machines
  • PCB drilling machines
  • Automated optical inspection (AOI)
  • Tunnel ovens
  • Circuit and solder mask printing
  • Visual inspection systems
  • Laboratory instruments
  • Production tooling and fixtures
  • Primary and auxiliary materials

PCBA

Electronics assembly (PCBA)

Equipment and supporting supplies for assembly lines.

  • Auxiliary equipment for PCBA lines
  • Inspection equipment
  • Production tooling and fixtures
  • Industrial protective supplies

Automatic drill bit regrinding machine

A Hong Kong group with a company incorporated in Brazil

IVAN INTERNATIONAL TRADER GROUP LTDA is the company incorporated in Brazil that is part of the IVAN group, headquartered in Hong Kong.

Backed by China's supply chain and the group's technical resources, we develop the Brazilian and South American markets, supplying machines, equipment and materials for printed circuit board fabrication and electronics assembly.

Our service model combines international supply, technical expertise and local support, identifying each customer's production needs and offering solutions that fit their reality.
